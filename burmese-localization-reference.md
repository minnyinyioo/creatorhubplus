# CreatorHubPlus Burmese Localisation Reference

## Official writing observations

The public Burmese-language pages reviewed from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Myanmar National Portal use concise, formal noun phrases for headings, explicit procedural wording for public-facing information, and polite but direct statements for notices and requirements. The localisation will adopt this clarity without copying government identity, claims, or political wording.

| Usage area | Localisation approach | Public-source reference |
|---|---|---|
| Section headings | Use short, formal Burmese noun phrases such as `ဝန်ဆောင်မှုများ` and `ကိုယ်ရေးအချက်အလက် မူဝါဒ` | [MOFA](https://www.mofa.gov.mm/) |
| Instructions | Use direct procedural construction: identify the requirement, prepare genuine information, then proceed with the next step | [Myanmar National Portal](https://myanmar.gov.mm/) |
| Consent and privacy | Use clear explanations of essential versus optional analytics functions, without legal overstatement | [Myanmar National Portal](https://myanmar.gov.mm/) |
| Service boundaries | State that approval remains with the platform and only truthful, verifiable information should be used | CreatorHubPlus service policy |
