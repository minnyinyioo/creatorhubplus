# iamthura Structure Reference

This review uses iamthura.com solely as a structural reference. CreatorHubPlus will retain its own logo, names, bilingual service copy, payment/verification workflow and functional routes.

| Reference pattern | CreatorHubPlus adaptation |
|---|---|
| Compact logo-led top navigation | Keep CreatorHubPlus wordmark, language switch and protected payment/case actions in a single concise header. |
| Split editorial hero | Pair the Payout Bridge service promise with a restrained case-route visual, without copying people photography or personal brand content. |
| Repeating capability strip | Use a compact, single-color service-path strip for platform earnings, payouts, account setup and address support. |
| Clear sectional rhythm | Use a consistent sequence of introduction, services, case path, trust, FAQ and contact sections with fixed content widths and restrained dividers. |
| Direct CTA hierarchy | Retain one primary “start a case/payment” route and one secondary “explore services” route; keep write actions behind login. |
| Dense, practical footer | Preserve privacy, terms, Cookie preferences, bilingual links and operational navigation in a structured footer. |

The target palette will use one CreatorHubPlus teal hue with neutral white/ink surfaces and tonal opacity only; clay, orange, blue and other competing accents will be removed from the primary product interface.
