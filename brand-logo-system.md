# CreatorHubPlus Logo System

## C+ Link

**C+ Link** is the single CreatorHubPlus brand symbol. A clean open **C** creates an immediate, international letterform; the precise **+** inside the symbol stands for an added connection, a verified next step, and practical creator support. The silhouette is intentionally simple enough to remain recognizable at favicon scale without gradients, textures, or decorative effects.

The standard wordmark is always written **creatorhubplus**. It uses lowercase letterforms for an approachable, compact international signature. The plus sign belongs only inside the independent C+ Link symbol and never appears in the wordmark.

| Asset | Purpose | Minimum use size | Colour |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `CreatorHubPlusMark` | Favicon, route center, compact navigation, app rail | 16 px | CreatorHubPlus teal or white |
| `CreatorHubPlusLockup` | Header, footer, payment, legal pages, expanded workspace | 132 px wide | Teal on light surfaces; white on deep teal |
| `favicon.svg` | Browser tab | 16 px | Teal C+ Link symbol |
| `icon-maskable.svg` | Installed web app | 48 px | White C, soft-teal plus on teal |

## Use Rules

Use the C+ Link symbol alone only where available space is limited. In normal navigation, page headers, and footer ownership areas, pair it with the **creatorhubplus** wordmark. Maintain clear space equal to the plus-sign height around the symbol. Do not distort, outline, shadow, gradient-fill, rotate, combine with third-party logos, or add secondary marks.

Third-party platform and payment logos are not part of the CreatorHubPlus Logo system. Keep them separate and subordinate as factual route evidence.
