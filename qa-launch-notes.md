# Launch-readiness QA notes

- The preview account center loaded in an authenticated browser session and displayed the current user context plus two existing payment requests. This session was not used to create a new account or insert test records.
- The account center exposed Export CSV, order search, status/date filters, and notification controls. A direct browser click attempt for Export CSV timed out at the browser-extension layer, so the download was verified through rendered DOM tests rather than a confirmed local file download.
- The payment route `/payment?service=account_setup` loaded in service-first order. It showed Account setup, KBZ Pay, and a receipt/submission path, but the selected provider was marked "RECIPIENT NOT YET PUBLISHED" and the amount showed "Quote required". This blocks a safe end-to-end payment-proof submission until an authorized merchant recipient and quote are published.
- The English `/terms` route loaded with six baseline sections covering service scope, truthful information, payment review, third-party platforms, refunds/contact, and changes/acceptable use.
- A separate test account could not be created through OAuth because browser takeover was unavailable; no fabricated database test data was inserted.

Responsive verification update: desktop and 375px mobile screenshots covered `/staff/review`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/my/privacy`, and `/my/terms`. The staff review queue stayed readable with the responsive single-column layout and compact filter tabs; the legal pages kept readable typography, wrapped headings, and visible footer/cross-links without horizontal overflow. The empty staff queue means the screenshot could not display a live request's quick buttons, but the rendered interaction test exercised the real detail-header buttons and mutation wiring.

Follow-up browser evidence: after reopening `/account` in the available authenticated preview session, the page loaded two verified orders and two review notifications. Clicking Export CSV succeeded in entering the visible `Preparing CSV…` loading state in the browser; the extension did not expose a downloadable-file confirmation afterward. The payment route remains blocked for a safe submission because the merchant recipient is unpublished and the selected service quote is required.

Final browser export check: in the authenticated account center, clicking Export CSV changed the button to `Preparing CSV…`; after the asynchronous download completed, the button returned to `Export CSV` and the visible toast read `2 orders exported as CSV.` This confirms the browser flow and success feedback, although the browser tooling did not expose the downloaded file path.

Myanmar typography verification: desktop screenshots of `/my`, `/my/privacy`, and `/my/terms` show the new Noto Serif Myanmar display stack with clearer headline rhythm and Padauk body copy with comfortable reading density. At 375px, Burmese headings wrap into readable lines and legal content remains legible without horizontal overflow. The cookie-consent modal remains readable, although its mobile overlay naturally covers part of the hero while open.

Myanmar locale verification: `/account?lang=my`, `/staff/review?lang=my`, and `/staff/pricing?lang=my` render at desktop and 375px. The locale-aware shell hook is active without changing the English UI copy. `/workspace?lang=my` still shows the existing Maximum update depth error after the first timer-resume guard, so the Workspace verification item remains open and this must be fixed before the typography checkpoint.

Post-fix typography verification: after guarding Workspace timer hydration and lifecycle persistence, `/workspace?lang=my` rendered successfully at desktop and 375px; the previous maximum-update-depth error no longer appeared in the screenshots. Burmese home, privacy, and terms pages show the Padauk body/Noto Serif Myanmar heading pairing with readable wrapping on mobile. The cookie-consent modal remains intentionally visible in the preview and does not indicate an application render error.
